The Genesis of the Name

The Radiance of Moonlight Over Parisian Limestone

Born from the French lunaire—celestial, quiet, bathed in silver illumination—Lunair Atelier was conceived during a midnight crossing of Pont des Arts. We observed how moonlight softened Paris’s sharp classical stone colonnades into luminous, breathless poetry.

We sought to transpose that exact optical grace into modern nuptial couture. Too often, bridal gowns feel architectural yet suffocating, or fluid yet lacking permanence. Lunair exists to harmonise structural Parisian rigor with incandescent, weightless romance.
