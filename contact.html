Questions & Protocols

Frequently Asked Inquiries

Everything you need to know prior to crossing our Cour d’Honneur.

How far in advance should I book my bridal or groom fitting? expand_more
We customarily recommend initiating your first consultation 4 to 8 months prior to your wedding ceremony to allow ample time for muslin toiles, secondary silk fittings, and hand-embroidered veil accents. For destination patrons on condensed timelines, an express 8-week couture service is available upon special approval by our Creative Director.
Is there a consultation fee for the initial appointment? expand_more
The 90-minute initial silhouette consultation and archival swatch degustation is completely complimentary upon invitation. Should you decide to proceed with commissioning a bespoke ensemble, a dedicated creation deposit is credited entirely toward the craft of your garment.
Can we book a simultaneous couple fitting for both Bride & Groom? expand_more
Yes, our Grand Salon Privé connects to an adjoining Groom Study. Couples wishing to experience their fitting day together while maintaining absolute visual surprise can be accommodated in secluded parallel salons, converging for champagne afterward in the private garden terrace.
Do you offer virtual consultations for overseas patrons? expand_more
Precisely. We conduct comprehensive high-definition video consultations with patrons residing in North America, Asia, and the Middle East. Prior to your video appointment, Lunair Atelier dispatches an archival presentation box containing our silk crepe swatches, Calais lace samples, and tailored measurement guides directly to your residence.
