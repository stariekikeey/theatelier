The Bespoke Bridal Journey

An intimate collaboration unfolding across our historic Parisian salon, ensuring unhurried perfection for your wedding day.

01
Initial Consultation

Private Vision & Champagne

A 90-minute styling salon with Head Designer Julien Moreau. Review archival sketches, drape raw silk swatches, and define your personal silhouette code.

Week 01 · Paris Salon
02
Architecture

Anatomical Toile Fitting

We sculpt a prototype in raw French calico cotton directly onto your body to pinpoint posture, seam dynamics, and effortless arm movement.
