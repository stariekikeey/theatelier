The Lunair Protocol

The Four Sacred Fittings

From initial fabric dégustation to the final pressing, each commission is an intimate dialogue between your personal aesthetic and the master cutter’s eye.

01 Stage I

The Silhouette & Cloth Dégustation

A private salon session over vintage Champagne. Explore 400+ archive swatches from Dormeuil, Scabal, and rare Biella wool merchants.

90 Minutes · Salon N°18
02 Stage II

38 Anatomical Measurements

Recording spine slope, collar drop, pitch of arms, and walking gait. A unique paper master pattern is drafted solely for your physique.

Individual Pattern Cut
03 Stage III

The Raw Basted Toile Fitting

The suit arrives in raw calico and white basting thread without lapels or lining. Henri Vaneau sculpts shoulder balance directly on your frame.

Pure Architectural Sculpture
04 Stage IV

The Handcraft & Final Pressing

Hand-sewn Milanese buttonholes, silk lining insertion, and artisanal steam shaping. Sealed in cedarwood nuptial travel casing.
